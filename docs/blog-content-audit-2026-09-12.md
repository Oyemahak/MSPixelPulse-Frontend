# MSPixelPulse published blog content audit

Audit date: 2026-09-12
Published articles reviewed: 94
Scope: intent ownership, duplication, usefulness, internal-link role, factual safety, indexability, and improvement priority

## Decision summary

- All 94 articles have unique slugs and titles and retain a distinct query or decision purpose.
- No article is merged, redirected, canonicalized to another article, or noindexed in this release. Destructive consolidation requires Search Console query, backlink, and conversion evidence that was not available in the repository audit.
- Eight early cornerstone articles were the weakest relative to their commercial importance and were materially expanded in this release.
- Eleven concise, high-value growth guides are the next editorial depth priority. Their current structured answers remain useful and indexable.
- The remaining articles stay indexed and are expanded only when Search Console or customer questions show demand. This avoids mass-produced padding and preserves the existing one-page-per-intent architecture.

## Overlap review

Three topic pairs need monitoring but do not currently answer the same intent:

- `local-seo-toronto-small-business-guide` is a broad operating guide; `local-seo-audit-gta-small-business` is an audit workflow.
- `mobile-friendly-website-design-local-businesses` covers the complete responsive experience; `mobile-call-to-action-design-local-business` covers one conversion component.
- `service-page-content-strategy-local-business` covers one page type; `content-clusters-local-service-website` covers the relationship between multiple pages.

If future Search Console data shows the same query set, impression pattern, and user outcome across a pair, consolidate into the stronger page and use one permanent redirect. Do not merge based only on shared words.

## Editorial next steps

1. Use Search Console page-query pairs to choose the next expansion; prioritize impressions with weak position or click-through rate and pages associated with qualified enquiries.
2. Add original MSPixelPulse evidence such as anonymized process observations, measured before/after technical findings, screenshots with permission, and clearly labelled project lessons.
3. Preserve one canonical destination per intent. Update modified dates only after meaningful visible changes.
4. Do not inflate word count for its own sake. A concise complete answer is stronger than repeated generic paragraphs.
