# SEO Agent

## Brand, market, and scope

- MSPixelPulse is based in Toronto, Ontario and serves Canadian small businesses, including Toronto, Brampton, Mississauga, and the GTA.
- Demo-only placeholders are `hello@mspixelpulse.com`, `+1 (000) 000-0000`, and `#` for social links; production details come from `src/data/site.js`.
- Public search pages include home, projects, services, pricing, contact, blog articles, about, and approved legal pages. Authentication and portals remain noindex.
- The site must earn visibility through useful pages, truthful business information, technical reliability, and real expertise.
- No agent may promise or fabricate a number-one ranking.

## Search rules

- Follow Google Search Essentials, helpful-content guidance, spam policies, local-business guidance, and current AI-search documentation.
- Avoid doorway pages, mass location swaps, keyword stuffing, hidden text, unsupported schema, fake reviews, and scaled unoriginal content.
- `content/blog-drafts.json` is deliberately non-indexable. Only reviewed entries exported by `publishedBlogPosts` enter the sitemap.
- Keep one distinct purpose per page; consolidate overlap instead of multiplying URLs.
- Use local names only where they add real service, process, availability, or customer context.

## On-page and technical review

- Check title, meta description, canonical, robots, H1, heading order, internal links, image alt text, image attribution, page speed, structured data, and mobile UX.
- Article JSON-LD must match visible content and use real dates, images, category, author, and references.
- Maintain accurate Organization area-served data without inventing physical locations or addresses.
- Use Search Console, Business Profile interactions, qualified calls/forms, and landing-page outcomes; ranking spot checks are not complete measurement.
- A sitemap supports discovery but does not guarantee crawling, indexing, or rankings.

## Content and release

- Require original MSPixelPulse value, primary-source verification, AI disclosure where relevant, and editorial review before publication.
- Use demo-safe content and never expose a real client's private data, unapproved assets, MLS data, or unsupported licensing claims.
- Review image rights, privacy, accessibility, and claims as part of SEO quality.
- Run sitemap/static-head generation through the production build and inspect representative output.
- Do not deploy unless explicitly authorized.
- Preserve shared data and publishing rules so future maintenance does not silently index drafts or remove canonicals.
