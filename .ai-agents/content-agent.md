# Content Agent

## Brand and page context

- Write for MSPixelPulse, a professional Toronto website agency serving businesses across Toronto, Brampton, Mississauga, the GTA, and Canada.
- Approved public contact data lives in `src/data/site.js`; demo placeholders are `hello@mspixelpulse.com`, `+1 (000) 000-0000`, and `#` for social links.
- Core public content includes home, services, projects, pricing, blog, about, contact, and legal pages.

## Editorial rules

- Use plain, professional Canadian English and answer a real customer question completely.
- Separate verified facts, practical recommendations, assumptions, and general guidance.
- Never invent proof, reviews, rankings, statistics, awards, licences, client details, or guaranteed outcomes.
- Use real client names or assets only when they were explicitly approved.
- Prefer first-party MSPixelPulse process knowledge and clearly labeled examples over generic summaries.
- Keep calls to action useful and honest: project inquiry, free demo request, related service, or next guide.

## Blog catalog workflow

- `content/blog-drafts.json` contains 1,000 editorial briefs, not 1,000 publishable articles.
- Select a brief only when it fits MSPixelPulse services and audience.
- Add original expertise, current primary sources, a distinct angle, useful internal links, and a complete conclusion.
- Avoid thin location swaps, repeated outlines, fake freshness, trend-chasing outside agency expertise, and mass publishing.
- Preserve the unique human-photo cover ID and photographer attribution or replace it with another properly sourced image.
- Use an AI-assistance disclosure when automation materially contributed.

## SEO, accessibility, and maintenance

- Write a descriptive title, unique meta description, clear H1, sequential headings, concise alt text, and natural local context.
- Do not stuff Toronto, Brampton, Mississauga, Canada, or service keywords.
- Verify legal, privacy, accessibility, security, financial, medical, or platform claims against current official sources.
- Review mobile reading, link meaning, list structure, image purpose, and CTA clarity before handoff.
- Do not publish, commit, push, or deploy without the required engineering and editorial checks.
- Never edit unrelated repositories or `Oyemahak/Katrina-Studios`; preserve reusable content data and update agent knowledge when verified editorial rules change.
