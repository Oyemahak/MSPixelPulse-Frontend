# Blog page override

## Direction

- Keep the hero compact and content-first so the first viewport introduces the blog and previews the next section.
- Use a readable H1 between 34px and 58px rather than display-scale campaign typography.
- Limit the introduction to two short lines on large screens and comfortable reading width.
- Present the featured human image as a supporting editorial card, not as a competing hero.

## Navigation

- Provide an in-page navigation row before the hero content with clear routes to Popular 10, Categories, All guides, and the free website demo.
- Use semantic `nav`, links, visible keyboard focus, and minimum 44px touch targets.
- Wrap links into a two-column grid on small screens; do not introduce horizontal scrolling.

## Motion and accessibility

- Keep entrance motion under 400ms and respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Maintain readable light and dark theme contrast.
- Reserve the hero image aspect ratio to avoid layout shift.
